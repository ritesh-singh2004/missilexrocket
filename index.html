DR. APJ ABDUL KALAM

The Missile Man of India

"If a country is to be corruption-free and become a nation of beautiful minds, I strongly feel there are three key societal members who can make a difference. They are the father, the mother, and the teacher."
- Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam (1931-2015), known as the "Missile Man of India", was the 11th President of India and a legendary aerospace scientist. He played a pivotal role in developing India's ballistic missile and launch vehicle technology.

SLV-III Project Director - India's first satellite launch vehicle
IGMDP Director - Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme
Agni & Prithvi Key architect of India's strategic missile programme
11th President People's President of India (2002-2007)

His legacy continues to inspire MissileX's mission to advance India's defence and space research capabilities.

1980

First indigenous orbital launch
