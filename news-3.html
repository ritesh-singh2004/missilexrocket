ANNOUNCEMENT

Research Fellowship 2026 cohort announced

02 July 2026

MissileXRocketSpace has announced its 2026 Research Fellowship cohort, selecting twenty-two fellows from over 300 applicants across India. The fellows will join structured research tracks in guidance systems, satellite analytics, and propulsion modelling over the next twelve months.

Selection Process

Applicants underwent a rigorous multi-stage evaluation including academic record review, technical aptitude assessment, research proposal evaluation, and personal interviews with senior scientists. The selection committee prioritised candidates demonstrating strong analytical skills, research potential, and alignment with MissileXRocketSpace's strategic research priorities.

The final cohort represents institutions from 14 states, with 40% of fellows coming from IITs and IISc, and the remainder from NITs, state universities, and defence research institutions. Notably, 35% of selected fellows are women, reflecting the organisation's commitment to gender diversity in aerospace research.

Research Tracks

The fellowship programme is organised into three primary research tracks. The Guidance Systems track focuses on developing autonomous navigation algorithms for next-generation launch vehicles, with emphasis on AI-driven trajectory optimisation. The Satellite Analytics track develops machine learning pipelines for Earth observation data processing, climate monitoring, and space situational awareness. The Propulsion Modelling track advances computational fluid dynamics simulation for liquid and solid rocket motor design.

Each fellow is assigned a primary mentor from the senior research staff and gains access to MissileXRocketSpace's simulation infrastructure, including the AI Mission Planning Laboratory and the Launch Vehicle Simulation Network.

Fellowship Structure

The twelve-month programme combines structured coursework with hands-on research. Fellows complete 200 hours of technical training covering advanced topics in aerospace engineering, computational methods, and research methodology. The remaining time is dedicated to their primary research project, which must produce a publishable result by the programme's conclusion.

Monthly progress reviews ensure fellows remain on track, while quarterly seminars provide opportunities to present findings to the broader research community. The programme culminates in a capstone symposium where fellows present their work to representatives from ISRO, DRDO, and partner institutions.

Outcomes and Expectations

Past fellowship cohorts have produced over 40 peer-reviewed publications, with three fellows subsequently joining MissileXRocketSpace's permanent research staff. The programme also serves as a pipeline for PhD candidates, with 25% of alumni progressing to doctoral programmes at leading institutions.

The 2026 cohort begins on 1 August 2026. Fellows receive a monthly stipend, access to laboratory facilities, and travel support for conference presentations. Applications for the 2027 cohort will open in January 2027.
